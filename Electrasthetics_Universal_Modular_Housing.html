Electrasthetics Product Proposal

Universal Modular Housing System

A coordinated family of surface-mounted housings designed to accept a wide range of campervan electrical modules while preserving one consistent visual language.

The flagship product family

The Universal Modular Housing System is conceived as the foundation of the Electrasthetics range. Instead of designing a separate enclosure for every switch, socket or control, the system creates a common housing architecture that can accept multiple module types through interchangeable fronts, inserts and adapter plates.

One exterior design. Multiple internal configurations. A product family that can expand without forcing converters to accept a mismatched collection of visible fittings.

The market problem

Campervan electrical installations are assembled from products made by unrelated manufacturers. Even where the equipment is technically excellent, the finished installation often contains conflicting shapes, colours, fixing methods and surface finishes.

A converter may need to combine round 12V sockets, rectangular rocker switches, USB modules, push buttons, voltmeters and CBE or Powerpart components within the same visible area. Current solutions rarely make those items look intentionally related.

Typical compromises

  • Visible screws and exposed fixings
  • Different bezel depths and corner radii
  • Black-only or very limited colour choice
  • Cut-outs made directly into finished cabinetry
  • Poor service access after installation
  • No consistent system for mixed module types

The Electrasthetics solution

The proposed system separates the visible housing from the electrical module it contains. This makes it possible to standardise the exterior appearance while adapting the interior geometry to suit each component.

Common BackplateProvides the mounting interface and hidden fixing structure.
Application InsertSupports the required switch, socket, control or adapter plate.
Snap-Fit HousingCreates the finished visible surface and shared product identity.

Housing variants

The Universal Modular range is proposed in three principal forms. Each shares the same core aesthetic but serves a different installation requirement.

Vertical Housing

For narrow wall spaces, furniture ends and stacked controls.

Horizontal Housing

For grouped sockets and controls above worktops, beds and seating areas.

Tilted Housing

For improved visibility and access beneath cupboards or on low surfaces.

Concealed mounting concept

The proposed installation system uses a separate backplate with hidden retention features. The visible housing clips into place after wiring and testing, leaving no exposed front screws.

Position
Set out the backplate and cable entry point.
Fix
Secure using appropriate mechanical fixings or 3M VHB where suitable.
Connect
Install and test the selected electrical module.
Finish
Clip the housing into place to conceal the mounting system.

Manufacturing logic

Designed in the UKDeveloped around real campervan installation problems rather than adapted from domestic products.
Rapid prototypingDimensions, clips and module fit can be refined quickly before committing to volume manufacture.
Shared componentsCommon backplates and housings reduce duplicated development across the range.
Batch productionAdditive manufacture supports low-volume launch and product validation.
Scalable rangeNew inserts can extend compatibility without redesigning the complete enclosure.
Future optionsSelected high-volume components could later move to alternative manufacturing processes.

Commercial value

The Universal Modular Housing System is commercially stronger than a one-off accessory because every additional compatible module increases the usefulness of the complete ecosystem.

Repeat purchasesCustomers can add matching components throughout a vehicle.
Installer loyaltyConverters can standardise around one visual system.
Low-risk expansionNew inserts can be introduced without replacing the core architecture.
Recognisable identityThe same housing language creates immediate brand recognition.
Cross-sector potentialSuitable for campervans, motorhomes, boats, trailers and specialist vehicles.
Range-building opportunityCreates a platform for future smart controls and specialist accessories.

Reference products and compatibility research

Existing market products are referenced for dimensional research and compatibility planning. Electrasthetics does not propose copying those products; the objective is to design original housings that accept widely used standard modules.

Development status

Product name, exact range placement, final dimensions, retention geometry and manufacturing specification remain under development. The purpose of this proposal is to demonstrate the system-level opportunity and provide a basis for selecting the first prototypes for commercial evaluation.
