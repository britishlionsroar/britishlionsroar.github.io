01
The platform

One housing language. Many electrical functions.

The universal modular housing is the core architecture behind the wider Electrasthetics range.

Instead of designing a completely new enclosure for every switch, socket and controller, the system separates the visible housing from the functional insert. This allows the exterior form, fixing method and finish to remain consistent while the internal adapter changes to suit the selected component.

The commercial value lies in reusing the same platform repeatedly while expanding compatibility through comparatively simple new inserts.

Five parts. One finished product.

Each layer has a defined job, allowing appearance, installation and module compatibility to be developed independently.

02
Concealed fixing

Secure in use. Clean from every viewing angle.

The preferred arrangement uses a lower hook rail and two concealed upper catches.

The installer engages the housing onto the lower rail, rotates it into position and presses until the catches lock. Removal is deliberate rather than accidental, but the finished face remains free of exposed screws.

1

Locate

Engage the lower edge of the housing with the backplate hook rail.

2

Rotate

Pivot the cover toward the surface while the lower rail controls alignment.

3

Lock

Twin concealed catches secure the upper edge without visible fasteners.

4

Service

Release the cover when access is required without damaging the installation.

Optional 3M VHB 5952 adhesive backing can support no-drill installation on suitable prepared surfaces.

04
Manufacturing logic

Develop the expensive geometry once. Extend the range through smaller parts.

The shared platform reduces the need to reinvent the complete enclosure for each new electrical function.

Early production can use additive manufacturing for low tooling cost, rapid revision and broad finish choice. The architecture should nevertheless be designed with future moulding or hybrid manufacture in mind, allowing successful products to scale without abandoning the original system.

Early-stage production

3D printing enables quick prototyping, short production runs, custom colours and rapid adapter development for new modules.

Volume-stage production

Common outer housings and backplates can later justify tooling, while lower-volume adapters remain printed or separately manufactured.

Standardisation improves inventory control: a limited number of housings can support a much larger catalogue of functional combinations.

05
Future expansion

A platform that becomes more valuable as the range grows.

New inserts, modules and outer forms can be added without invalidating the products already developed.

This creates room for later compatibility with smart controls, sensor modules, touch interfaces, branded converter panels and specialist products for marine, horsebox and expedition vehicle markets.

A

New adapters

Add compatibility with emerging electrical modules while retaining the same outer housing.

B

New formats

Develop single, double, triple and wider control-panel versions from the same core logic.

C

New finishes

Release coordinated colour collections without changing the underlying engineering.

D

New markets

Apply the architecture to boats, caravans, horseboxes and other compact specialist interiors.
