01
The concept

From scattered switches to one considered control point.

Vehicle electrical controls are often assembled from unrelated parts with inconsistent spacing, colours and fixing methods.

Electrasthetics modular control panels provide a structured alternative: one coordinated enclosure that can accept different combinations of switches, sockets, dimmers, push buttons and indicator modules.

The customer chooses the function. Electrasthetics controls the appearance, spacing and installation quality.

Architecture

A repeatable framework for custom configurations.

Each finished panel is assembled from a limited family of shared parts rather than designed from scratch.

01

Common backplate

Provides the fixing points, cable clearances and structural base for the complete assembly.

02

Grid carrier

Establishes consistent module spacing and supports multiple adapter formats.

03

Module adapters

Accept round, rectangular and specialist controls within the same panel geometry.

04

Decorative fascia

Conceals all fixing points and gives the panel its finished colour, texture and edge profile.

Panel layouts

Standard footprints with configurable internals.

A small number of panel sizes can cover most practical installations while still allowing customised control combinations.

Compact Two-Way

For paired lighting, water pump, heater or charging controls in tight spaces.

Four-Way Utility

A general-purpose control point for the most commonly used vehicle systems.

Six-Way System Panel

For larger conversions needing a clear central location for multiple circuits and functions.

Custom legends, icon sets and pre-wired configurations can create converter-specific or customer-specific variants without changing the core panel system.

Commercial value

A product family with both retail and trade potential.

Modular control panels can serve self-build customers, professional converters and branded trade accounts from the same underlying parts.

Retail configurations

Customers can buy empty housings, standard populated kits or coordinated control packs for common van systems.

Trade configurations

Converters can specify repeatable panel layouts, custom legends and pre-wired harnesses under their own agreed specification.

Every additional compatible switch or control expands the catalogue without requiring a completely new enclosure.
