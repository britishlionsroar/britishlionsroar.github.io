Rebuild Britain's Armed Forces

Our Armed Forces have been cut to the bone.

We believe in peace through strength.

We will rebuild Britain's armed forces, invest in capability and readiness, end woke, and restore morale across the military and intelligence services.

We will ensure our armed forces are ready to deal with the ever-present threats posed by Russia and China.

Britain must once again be respected by allies and feared by adversaries.

Supporting detail: defence funding in the Economic Rescue Plan

Reform UK’s Economic Rescue Plan (October 2026), printed page 17, “Additional Expenditure”:

We have also specifically allocated funding to meet the shortfall in the Defence Investment Plan and to scrap tax on reservists’ pay. This is only the beginning of our commitment to defence expenditure, which we will lay out in more detail in the near future. Our unallocated spending allowance — which is on top of Labour’s existing spending plans — includes ample room to meet the 3% by 2030 defence spending target. It also provides room for investment in securing our borders and making our streets safer.
