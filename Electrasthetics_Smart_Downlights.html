Chapter 07

Smart Downlights

A premium modular lighting system for campervans, motorhomes and specialist vehicles.

Concept

The Smart Downlight range is designed around one premium housing with interchangeable bezels, finishes and control options. The goal is to offer lighting that looks like architectural interior lighting rather than generic caravan fittings.

Core Features

Premium Appearance

Flush, modern styling with coordinated finishes.

Smart Control

Compatible with dimming, automation and app control.

High Efficiency

Low power consumption with high light output.

Modular Design

Shared components reduce manufacturing cost.

Commercial Opportunity

Lighting is one of the most visible upgrades in any conversion. A coordinated premium range strengthens the Electrasthetics ecosystem and encourages repeat purchases across the wider product family.
