★ Bringing House-Style Smart Lighting to VanLifers ★

Real 230V Smart Lighting.
In a Van, For the First Time.

Until now, RGB, dimmable-white, voice-controlled, music-reactive downlighting was something only buildings, motorhomes and caravans could have — because only they had a real ceiling cavity to fit a proper 230V downlight into. This housing brings that same fitting to campervans, surface-mounted, secure, and colour-coordinated.

Original commercial LED downlight, the housing, and the light mounted inside it

Not just a nicer surround. The first way to fit real smart lighting in a van at all.

The Gap This Closes
Campervans have been stuck with weak 12V puck lights.

Without a ceiling cavity to fit a proper 230V downlight into, campervan owners have been limited to roughly 3W stick-on 12V pucks, or LED strips with no true dimmable white light. Full house-style smart lighting — RGB, dimmable white, Alexa or app control, music-reactive — needed a cavity that campervans simply don't have.

What This Housing Does
A cavity, built into the surface fitting itself.

This housing surface-mounts a genuine 230V, 10W smart downlight — the same fitting a house or motorhome would use — without needing to build a ceiling cavity or box in ugly trunking. It gives that fitting a secure, coordinated, colour-matched home for the first time in a campervan.

How It's Built
Two ends, two different jobs.

The housing works differently at each end, because each end is solving a different problem — fixing to the roof at one end, presenting and holding the light at the other.

ROOF END — HIDDEN

Inward-Facing Access Bezel

Faces in towards the access hole so hands and wiring can pass through during installation. Once the housing is fixed to the roof, this end is fully concealed — it's never seen. Fixed using either a VHB tape ring for a permanent bond, or A&B self-adhesive industrial Velcro rings, which let the whole light and housing be released from the roof if it's ever needed.

DOWN END — VISIBLE

Internal Retaining Ridge

An internal ridge roughly two-thirds of the way up the tube grips the light fitting's own spring clips, holding it snugly in place — the same way it would sit in a standard cut ceiling hole. The light comes straight out downward for servicing, without touching the roof fixing at all.

Power & Control
230V lighting, run sensibly off a 12V system.

These are genuine 230V, 10W smart downlights, run through a 230V pure sine inverter — the same fittings a house would use, not a 12V-only compromise. Running an inverter does introduce a standby draw, typically 7-18W, even while the lights themselves are dimmed or off. Left on permanently, that adds up.

In practice this is straightforward to manage. A VanLifers inline voice/remote control module fitted in the inverter's own feed lets it be switched on only when needed, rather than left running continuously. Alternatively, these lights can be wired to the hookup/shore-power feed only, so they simply work whenever the van's plugged in — with the option to also run them from the inverter when off-grid. A van with a decent solar setup or a larger LiFePO4 battery bank will find the standby draw negligible either way.

Optional Extra
Covering the light's own bezel — if you want to.

At the visible, down-facing end, the original light fitting's own bezel remains on display — typically white. Electrasthetics offers this as a peel-and-stick High-Grade Cast Vinyl ring, sized to match, in the same colour range as the rest of the housing. The customer decides: leave the original bezel visible, match it to the housing colour, or choose a contrasting colour.
