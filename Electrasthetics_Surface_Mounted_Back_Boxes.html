★ For Finished Cabinetry ★

No Cutting.
No Compromise.

Surface Mounted Back Boxes bring a clean, coordinated finish to installations where cutting into finished furniture isn't practical or possible — retrofits, upgrades, and anywhere the cabinetry has already been built.

Surface Mounted Back Box housing a 12V/24V control panel

Not every installation can cut into the wall. This one doesn't need to.

What It Is
A backbox that sits on top of the surface.

Rather than requiring a cavity cut into cabinetry or wall panel, the Surface Mounted Back Box fixes directly to the finished surface. It uses the same Universal Modular Housing architecture and insert system as the rest of the range, so it accepts the same range of functional modules.

Where It's Used
Retrofits and finished furniture.

Ideal for adding sockets, switches or controls to a van that's already built, upgrading an existing installation, or any position where cutting a cavity would risk damaging structural cabinetry or existing wiring behind the panel.

Cut-In vs Surface Mounted
Two mounting approaches, one coordinated look.

Cut-in housings sit flush with the surface but need cavity work. Surface mounted housings need no cutting, but must be shaped to sit cleanly proud of the surface rather than looking like an afterthought.

Cut-In Only Housings

❌ Requires cutting into finished cabinetry
❌ Risk of hitting structural framing or existing wiring
❌ Difficult or impossible on some retrofits
❌ Not reversible without visible repair
