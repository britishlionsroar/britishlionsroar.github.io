01
The problem

Most surface boxes are designed to be tolerated, not displayed.

Existing products are typically utility enclosures: deep, visually heavy, limited in colour and secured with exposed screws.

They may function adequately, but they rarely complement premium cabinetry or the carefully designed interiors now common in modern conversions. Many also offer poor compatibility with the mixed low-voltage components used in campervans.

The gap is not simply for another backbox. It is for a surface-mounted finishing system that belongs in the living space.

03
Installation

Four straightforward stages from surface to finished product.

1

Prepare

Select the module insert, cable entry and appropriate mounting method for the surface.

2

Mount

Secure the backplate using adhesive, screws or a combined fixing method.

3

Wire

Connect the electrical component while access remains open and unrestricted.

4

Close

Engage the lower hook rail and lock the outer housing onto the concealed catches.

The housing can be removed later for maintenance without pulling adhesive from the wall or disturbing the wiring backplate.

05
Commercial case

A straightforward launch product with platform value.

Surface-mounted back boxes are easy to understand, widely applicable and well suited to early additive manufacturing.

The product can be sold individually, in matched sets or as part of pre-configured electrical packs. Professional converters gain a repeatable finishing component, while self-builders gain a cleaner alternative to generic utility boxes.

Direct product opportunity

Single, double and triple enclosures can generate sales across upgrades, new conversions and replacement installations.

Platform opportunity

Each backbox sale creates demand for compatible inserts, matching control panels and future Electrasthetics products.

This range is suitable as an early flagship because the visual improvement is immediate and easily demonstrated in photographs, prototypes and installer videos.
