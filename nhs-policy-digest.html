Reform UK and the NHS

What Reform UK’s published policy documents say about the NHS, doctors, nurses and health, quoted word for word.

About this page. Reform UK has not published a single NHS policy paper. Its NHS commitments are spread across several documents. This page brings them together. Every extract is quoted word for word from the source document. Each one shows the page it comes from, with links to read it in context, open the full document, and check the Reform UK original.

This is a compilation by The British Lions Roar, not an official Reform UK document. Pledges from the 2024 general-election document are kept separate, in the final section.

As of 28 October 2026, Reform UK has not published a standalone NHS policy paper. None of the current documents in this library sets out policy on NHS waiting lists, GP access, A&E or social care.

The NHS measures above are spread across documents on education, the economy, welfare and immigration. This page collects them in one place.

92024 manifesto pledges, not repeated in current policy

These pledges come from Our Contract with You, Reform UK’s June 2024 general-election document. They are kept here for reference. They do not appear in Reform UK’s current policy documents, and some differ from current policy. For example, the 2024 document proposed writing off student fees over 10 years of NHS service, while current policy proposes 0% interest loans for nurses.
