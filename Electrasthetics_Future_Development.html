★ Where the System Can Go Next ★

Fourteen Chapters Is
the Start, Not the Ceiling.

The architecture behind this range — shared closures, shared finishes, shared insert logic — was built to expand. This chapter sets out where that expansion could go once the first phase is established.

The hard part was building the system. Adding to it should be the easy part.

Expansion Categories
Further products, the same shared architecture.

Every product proposed here shares the same closure system, insert logic and finish range. That makes further expansion a matter of designing a new housing shape, not rebuilding the whole system from scratch each time.

01

Camera Mounting Pods

Bonded or magnetic mounts for compact battery security cameras, extending the range to exterior surveillance.

02

Roof-Channel Adaptor Plates

Coordinated adaptors for roof fans, skylights, solar feet and cable glands where they pass through the roof.

03

Smart Control & Timer Relay Housings

Housings for Tuya-style smart modules, DC timers and relays, extending automation into the coordinated finish.

04

Pre-Fitted Control Boxes

Populated, pre-wired versions of the modular housing range for converters who want a ready-to-fit unit.

05

Installation Brackets & Trim

Supporting clips, brackets and finishing parts that round out a complete installation.

06

Further Insert Types

New functional inserts added to the existing Universal Modular Housing without a new housing design.

A Longer-Term Idea
An online configurator, once the range is established.

A "build your wiring kit" tool — where a customer enters their van, roof fan and floorplan details, and the tool calculates and baskets the trunking, corners, switches and modules needed automatically. This is a later-stage feature, not something to build now, but the shared architecture across the range is what would make it possible.

Why Start Narrow
Prove the system first. Scale it second.

The right approach is to validate a focused first range properly, rather than launching wide and thin. Every category above builds on the same architecture already proposed here — none of it requires starting again.

Why It Matters
The value compounds with every product added.
A

Low Marginal Cost

New products reuse the same closure, finish and insert systems rather than starting from zero.

B

Stronger With Scale

Every product added increases the reason for a customer to standardise on the whole range.

C

Clear Long-Term Direction

Gives Anvil a roadmap to grow into, without needing every decision made today.

The system was built to grow. This is what growing it could look like.
