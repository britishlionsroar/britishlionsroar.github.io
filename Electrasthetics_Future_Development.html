01
Development strategy

Expand from a disciplined core rather than chasing unrelated products.

The strongest route forward is to build outward from the shared Electrasthetics design language, modular mounting system and vehicle-specific installation knowledge already established.

New products should either fit the existing visual family, solve an adjacent installation problem or increase the value of products already in the range. That keeps development focused and makes every new release strengthen the brand as a whole.

The objective is not to manufacture everything used in a vehicle. It is to own the visible interface between technical hardware and the finished interior.

Product pipeline

Natural extensions already exist around the proposed range.

Climate

Fan, vent and skylight adaptors

Adaptor plates for different roof ribs, trims and aperture sizes across popular roof-mounted equipment.

Security

External camera mounts

Vehicle-specific camera pods, roof mounts, magnetic bases and protective housings for compact security cameras.

Connectivity

Aerial and data modules

Roof-entry housings, antenna combiners, Ethernet outlets and complete router installation packages.

Control

Smart switching accessories

Relay housings, timer controls, sensor modules and app-connected control products using the same mounting system.

Lighting

Expanded luminaire family

Reading lights, task lights, strip-light terminations and surface luminaires coordinated with the smart downlight range.

Services

Utility monitoring housings

Displays and sensor housings for water, waste, battery, temperature, gas and other vehicle systems.

Platform expansion

The same architecture can create value beyond individual products.

Compatibility

Model-Specific Insert Library

A growing database of cradles, bezels and adaptor plates for popular third-party switches, gauges, routers and controllers.

Trade

Converter Specification System

A structured catalogue allowing professional converters to specify finishes, module layouts and compatible accessories across an entire build.

Digital

Online Configuration Tool

A future customer-facing interface for selecting housings, finishes, modules and combinations before ordering.

Manufacturing

Distributed Production

Approved digital manufacturing files and quality controls could support regional production without large central stockholding.

OEM

White-Label Partnerships

Selected products could be adapted for vehicle builders, equipment manufacturers and specialist distributors.

Aftermarket

Upgrade and Replacement Range

New fascias, inserts and covers allow owners to refresh appearance or change equipment without rebuilding the installation.

The long-term commercial asset is the compatibility system and design platform, not only the individual printed component.

03
Development roadmap

A staged route reduces risk and keeps development commercially grounded.

1

Validate the core range

Prototype the highest-value housings, test fit, confirm materials and gather installer feedback.

2

Launch compatible families

Release coordinated back boxes, control panels, housings and cable-management products.

3

Build the compatibility library

Add inserts and adaptors for more third-party products while preserving common outer formats.

4

Scale production and partnerships

Move suitable volumes to more efficient processes and secure trade, distributor or OEM relationships.

04
Commercial priorities

Future work should be filtered through clear commercial tests.

TestQuestionReason
Market gapDoes the product solve a problem not already served well?Avoid competing only on price
Range fitDoes it strengthen the Electrasthetics visual or mounting system?Build brand recognition and cross-sales
Production fitCan it be prototyped and produced economically at expected volume?Protect margin and cash flow
Installation valueDoes it save time, improve finish or simplify servicing?Create clear trade and retail benefits
Compatibility depthCan one outer product support several devices or variants?Increase return on development effort
DefensibilityIs the value in the complete system, fit data and presentation rather than a simple shape?Reduce easy imitation
Expansion potentialCan the product lead naturally to related accessories?Increase lifetime customer value
Each future product should earn its place by making the wider platform more useful, more recognisable or more commercially valuable.
