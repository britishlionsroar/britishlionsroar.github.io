Market Opportunity

The campervan market has evolved rapidly, with premium furniture, lithium power systems and sophisticated electronics now common across new builds. However, electrical fittings are still typically sourced from numerous unrelated manufacturers, resulting in inconsistent appearance throughout otherwise high-quality interiors.

Electrasthetics aims to solve this by providing a coordinated family of housings, mounting systems and finishing accessories that create a unified interior design language while remaining compatible with established electrical equipment.

Why the Opportunity Exists

  • Premium converters increasingly focus on interior aesthetics.
  • No manufacturer currently offers a complete coordinated electrical finishing system.
  • Installers mix products from multiple brands with differing colours, textures and fixing methods.
  • A modular ecosystem allows continual expansion without redesigning the entire range.

Commercial Benefits

  • Complements existing products rather than competing with them.
  • Low-cost entry into an expandable product ecosystem.
  • Suitable for retail customers, converters and OEM manufacturers.
  • Designed around scalable additive manufacturing.
