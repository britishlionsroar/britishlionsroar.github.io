01
The problem

Visible wiring is one of the fastest ways to make a conversion look unfinished.

Surface wiring is often unavoidable in retrofit work, thin wall constructions and areas where future service access matters. Existing trunking is usually designed for domestic or industrial buildings rather than compact, highly finished vehicle interiors.

Standard plastic channel tends to be bulky, stark white and visually disconnected from sockets, switches and control housings. Corners, branches and exits often look improvised, while screw heads and uneven cuts remain visible.

The opportunity is to treat cable routing as part of the interior design system rather than something to hide as cheaply as possible.

The solution

A modular surface-routing system built for vehicle-scale installations.

Electrasthetics Cable Management would use slim backplates, clip-on decorative covers and purpose-designed junction pieces to create a clean, serviceable wiring route.

01

Low-profile channel

Holds one or more cables securely while minimising visual bulk on walls, ceilings and cabinetry.

02

Purpose-designed fittings

Internal corners, external corners, tees, end caps and equipment transitions remove the need for rough hand-cut joints.

03

Coordinated finish

Colours, textures and decorative faces match the wider Electrasthetics socket, switch and housing family.

03
Installation

Fast to fit, easy to reopen and designed to remain tidy.

1

Plan the route

Select the channel size, fittings and equipment transitions before cutting or fixing components.

2

Fix the backplate

Use concealed screws, structural adhesive tape or a combined method appropriate to the surface.

3

Lay and retain cables

Separate and secure conductors with internal clips, dividers or tie points where required.

4

Apply covers and fittings

Clip on the decorative face, corners and transitions to create one continuous finished system.

Commercial value

A supporting range that strengthens every other product.

Cable management may be less glamorous than a control panel or smart light, but it solves the installation problem that sits between those products.

Retail

Retrofit-friendly upgrade

Owners can add lighting, controls and accessories without leaving loose or visibly improvised wiring.

Trade

Reduced finishing time

Converters gain repeatable parts for common routing problems, reducing one-off fabrication and rework.

Platform

Ecosystem connector

Purpose-designed transitions physically and visually link the wider Electrasthetics product family.

Cable Management is the connective tissue of the range: modest as a standalone product, but strategically important to the whole system.
