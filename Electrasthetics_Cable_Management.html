★ TPU Trunking, Rolled Not Rigid ★

No More Rigid Lengths.
No More Visible Seams.

A 3D printed TPU trunking system, manufactured as continuous, seamless rolls rather than short rigid sections — coiling flat for storage and popping straight back to its true shape when it's time to fit.

Flat-backed TPU trunking, coiled in a roll — true shape memory means it pops back to a clean 90° V-back once unrolled

A material that remembers its shape. A joint that locks without a single screw.

Why TPU
A true elastomer, not rigid plastic.

TPU behaves like heavy-duty rubber, with genuine shape memory. It can be coiled tightly for shipping, sit folded in a cupboard for months, and still return cleanly to its true 90° V-back shape the moment it's unrolled — something a rigid plastic profile simply can't do.

Why Rolls, Not Rigid Lengths
Built for van storage, not warehouse shelving.

Manufactured as continuous 5-metre or 10-metre rolls via industrial-scale 3D belt printing or custom silicone extrusion moulding, rather than short 3-metre rigid lengths. That solves two problems at once — no awkward long lengths to store in a van, and no unsightly seams from clipping short sections together.

How It Closes
A living hinge on one side, a self-locking wedge on the other.

One edge uses a living hinge — a thin flexible strip of the same material, calibrated to 0.6-0.8mm in TPU 95A, that folds completely flat for rolling, opens wide for cable clip access, and won't snap or fatigue with repeated use. The opposite edge uses a male-to-female self-locking wedge joint: a hollow wedge receiver on the V-back base meets a male spread wedge on the D-cover, so closing the lid presses firmly against a solid, taped wall surface for a clean, satisfying lock.

01

Mounting

3M VHB tape pre-applied to both outer flat faces of the V-back — the tape's elastic foam core flexes and rolls with the trunking without peeling away in transit.

02

Cable Retention

Peel-and-stick flat-backed cable clips, applied by the customer inside the flat back walls — larger clips for insulated 230V mains, smaller for 12V lighting cable.

03

Product Split

Offered as separate sizes for 12V/24V and 230V circuits, matching the wiring it's actually meant to carry.
