The problem with fixed solar
Traditional mounts have
one fundamental limitation.

They Never Move.

Every conventional roof-mounted solar panel is fixed permanently in one position. During summer this works reasonably well. During winter, as the sun drops to just 10–20° above the horizon, a flat panel simply cannot intercept that low-angle sunlight efficiently. This is not opinion. It is basic geometry.

Winter is when every watt counts.

In December and January, the UK sun sits just 10–20° above the horizon at midday. A flat panel catches almost none of that direct winter light. The SolarRaise™ Winter preset raises the panel to 60–65°, directly intercepting low-angle winter sunlight.

Annually, a flat panel loses 15–25% compared to optimal tilt. In the depths of winter, on a clear day, SolarRaise™ OneTouch can recover up to 78% more output from the same panel.

Aerodynamics
Integrated wind deflector.
Engineered for the road.

Many raised mounting brackets leave a large unsupported air gap beneath the leading edge of the solar panel. At motorway speeds this creates turbulence, wind noise and aerodynamic drag. SolarRaise™ incorporates a full-width leading-edge wind deflector integrated directly into the carrier assembly — forming part of the primary structure, not an optional accessory.

✅

Reduces aerodynamic turbulence and wind noise

✅

Protects the leading edge of the panel

✅

Contributes to structural stiffness

✅

Visually integrated — part of the product, not an afterthought
