Electrasthetics™

A Complete Interior Electrical Aesthetics System

Commercial Product Proposal for Anvil

Executive Summary

Electrasthetics is a proposed ecosystem of coordinated electrical housings, mounting systems and installation accessories designed specifically for campervans, motorhomes and marine applications. Rather than producing isolated accessories, the concept introduces a unified design language intended to give every visible electrical fitting a consistent, premium appearance.

This proposal is presented as a commercial development proposal rather than a finished catalogue. Product naming, manufacturing methods, packaging and final retail positioning will be refined alongside Anvil as preferred product families are selected.

Core Objectives

Unified Appearance
Replace mismatched fittings with one coordinated aesthetic.
Modular Design
Shared mounting philosophy across multiple product families.
Hidden Fixings
Reduce visible screws and improve installation finish.
Expandable Ecosystem
A platform capable of growing into a complete interior system.
