★ A Domestic Fitting, Adapted for Thin Doors ★

Any Digital Door Viewer.
Now Fits a Van Door.

Domestic digital door viewers, like those from Yale, are built for house door thicknesses. This adapter kit closes the gap so the same fitting can be installed properly in the much thinner doors found on campervans and caravans.

Electrasthetics digital door viewer thickness adaptor kit

The viewer isn't the problem. The door thickness is — until now.

The Problem
House doors and van doors aren't the same thickness.

Digital door viewers are designed and manufactured around standard domestic door thicknesses. Campervan and caravan doors are typically much thinner, meaning these fittings often can't be installed as-is, or sit poorly if forced in.

The Solution
A spacer/adapter kit, not a new viewer to design.

Rather than developing a bespoke thin-door viewer from scratch, this kit adapts the thickness gap — letting a proven, widely available domestic digital viewer be fitted correctly and securely into a thinner van or caravan door.

Why It Matters
A small product solving a real, specific problem.
A

Uses Proven Hardware

No need to source, test or support a bespoke door viewer — the adapter does the work.

B

Genuine Security Upgrade

Digital door viewers offer real benefits over a standard peephole, now genuinely fittable in a van door.

C

Low Development Cost

A simple, focused adapter part rather than a complex housing — quick to bring to market.
