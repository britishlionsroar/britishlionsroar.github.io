01
The problem

Vehicle doors rarely provide a safe, discreet way to see who is outside.

Conventional domestic peepholes are poorly suited to insulated vehicle doors, while generic cameras often create exposed wiring, awkward brackets and an installation that looks added on.

Campervan and motorhome owners may also want to check outside without opening blinds, switching on exterior lights or revealing that the vehicle is occupied.

The opportunity is to package proven camera and display hardware into a vehicle-specific, visually coordinated installation kit.

Product range

Three versions cover simple viewing through to connected security.

EA-DV1

Local Viewer Kit

A straightforward camera and interior screen for immediate door-side viewing without cloud services or mobile connectivity.

EA-DV2

Recording Viewer Kit

Adds local image or video storage, motion activation and optional low-light illumination.

EA-DV3

Connected Viewer Kit

Supports mobile alerts, remote viewing and integration with a wider vehicle security or connectivity system.

03
Installation

Designed around the realities of vehicle doors.

1

Survey the door

Confirm panel thickness, insulation depth, reinforcement and the safest cable route.

2

Fit the exterior pod

Mount the camera through a sealed plate designed to spread load and resist water ingress.

3

Route power and data

Use a protected passage through the door structure and flexible transfer where required.

4

Install the interior display

Secure the coordinated housing at a practical viewing height with concealed fixings.
