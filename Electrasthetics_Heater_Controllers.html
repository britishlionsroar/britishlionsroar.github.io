Chapter 08

Heater Controllers

Premium housings and coordinated control interfaces for diesel, gas and electric heating systems—designed to bring one of the most visually awkward vehicle components into the Electrasthetics family.

Cabin temperature21°C

01
The problem

Heating controllers often look added on rather than designed in.

Even premium heaters are commonly supplied with controller bodies that clash with cabinetry, sit proud of the wall or expose awkward wiring and mounting details.

Different brands use different shapes, cut-outs and cable arrangements, forcing converters to improvise one-off mounting plates or accept a visually inconsistent finish.

The opportunity is to standardise the installation around a premium outer housing while retaining the proven OEM controller inside.
02
The solution

A coordinated housing around the controller customers already trust.

Electrasthetics does not need to replace heater electronics. It can improve how those electronics are mounted, protected and presented.

01

Decorative fascia

Creates a premium outer form matching sockets, switches and control panels elsewhere in the vehicle.

02

Brand-specific insert

Adapts the common housing to the exact OEM controller geometry and cable route.

03

Shared backplate

Provides a repeatable fixing method for surface-mounted or partially recessed installations.

Product range

Three launch formats cover the main installation types.

EA-HC1

Compact Surface Housing

For small digital controllers where no wall void is available and wiring must remain accessible.

EA-HC2

Semi-Recessed Fascia

Uses a shallow wall cut-out to reduce projection while retaining a serviceable outer frame.

EA-HC3

Integrated Panel Insert

Allows a heater controller to sit within a wider Electrasthetics control panel alongside related switches and indicators.

The same outer form can support multiple heater brands through replaceable controller-specific inserts.
03

Designed around real vehicle systems.

The housing family can accommodate control units used with diesel air heaters, diesel water heaters, gas heating and electric auxiliary systems.

Commercial value

A small product with strong perceived value.

Heating controllers are visible, frequently used and usually installed at eye level, making the improvement immediately noticeable.

Retail

Upgrade accessory

Owners can improve an existing heater installation without replacing the heater or controller.

Trade

Converter standardisation

Professional builders can use repeatable branded housings across multiple heater systems.

Platform

Cross-sell potential
