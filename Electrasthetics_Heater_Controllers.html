The Controller Everyone Fits.
Finally Housed Properly.

A coordinated surface housing for digital heater controllers such as the Truma CP Plus and JP Heater panel — fittings that appear in almost every conversion, and that the market has never given a proper mounting solution.

Electrasthetics heater controller housing, surface mounted

Nearly every van has one of these. None of them have looked coordinated — until now.

What It Is
A proper housing for the controller you already fit.

Digital heater controllers like the Truma CP Plus and JP Heater panel are close to universal across conversions, but sit exposed and unmatched against the surrounding cabinetry. This housing wraps them in the same coordinated Electrasthetics finish as the rest of the installation.

The Market Gap
No real surface-mount option currently exists.

There is no genuine surface-mounted back box for this controller on the market today — only a back surround designed to be screwed directly to the surface. This is a straightforward gap Electrasthetics can fill properly.
