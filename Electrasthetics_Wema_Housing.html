What It Is
A proper enclosure for a small but essential instrument.

Wema voltage stabiliser instruments are small, functional, and easy to overlook when planning a finish — but left bare or loosely surface-fixed, they stand out against a coordinated interior just as much as any larger fitting.

Why It's Worth Doing
Small products still need a finished look.

A coordinated housing for even the smallest instruments reinforces the idea that every visible electrical component in the van belongs to the same system — not just the large, obvious ones.
