01
The problem

Useful instruments are often let down by poor presentation.

Wema-style gauges are widely used because they are compact, familiar and reliable, but they are normally mounted as isolated components with exposed fixings and little relationship to the surrounding interior.

Where several gauges are fitted together, alignment and spacing are often inconsistent. Surface installations can look particularly improvised when no suitable recessed cavity exists behind the wall or cabinet face.

The opportunity is not to redesign the gauge. It is to create a premium, repeatable way to mount and group it.

The solution

A coordinated outer housing for proven round instrumentation.

The Wema Housing uses a common outer form with replaceable gauge inserts, allowing the same visual product to support different round instrument sizes and layouts.

01

Single-gauge housing

Creates a clean standalone installation with concealed fixings and controlled cable entry.

02

Multi-gauge array

Aligns two, three or more instruments within one coherent fascia rather than separate cut-outs.

03

Universal insert platform

Supports different gauge diameters without changing the visible outer housing family.
