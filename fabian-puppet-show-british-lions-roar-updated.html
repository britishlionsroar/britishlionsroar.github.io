THE FABIAN SOCIETY AND THE UNIPARTY

WELCOME TO THE PUPPET SHOW

Behind the curtain of the Labour Party sits the Fabian machine.

Buckle up and get your tinfoil hat on — then keep the receipts beside you. This one will knock your socks off.

Political satire poster titled Welcome to the Puppet Show, showing a wolf in sheep’s clothing behind marionette politicians on a theatre stage.

Britain is told that Labour policy is created openly by elected politicians responding to the public.

Look behind the curtain and a very different picture appears.

The Fabian Society publishes Plans for Power. Its ideas are woven into Labour manifestos. Its staff brief civil servants. Its members fill Parliament and the Cabinet. People trained through its networks turn up inside No. 10 and beside Cabinet ministers. Its senior figures sit in city halls, mayoral offices, the Lords, unions, think tanks and public bodies.

Then Labour ministers walk onto the stage and announce the finished programme as though it appeared from nowhere.

The Labour Party performs on the stage.

The Fabian Society helps write the script behind the curtain.

START AT THE TOP — THE PRIME MINISTER IS ONE OF THEM

Before any of the advisers, the deputy mayors or the training schemes, start with the fact sitting at the top of the pyramid.

Keir Starmer is the Prime Minister of the United Kingdom.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-28) The Fabian Society's own account says every Labour prime minister in history has belonged to the Society, and its report for the year Labour returned to office said the prime minister and half the Cabinet were Fabian members.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-3)[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-5)

Put those two facts together and there is no ambiguity left to hide behind: by the Society's own words, the man in Downing Street is one of them.

Everything that follows in this article — the advisers, the deputy mayors, the training pipelines, the war room against Reform — sits underneath that single fact.

This does not start with special advisers or people standing near power. It starts at the top, in Downing Street.

THEY PUT THE METHOD ON THE LOGO

One of the Fabian Society’s most famous early emblems showed a black wolf in white sheep’s clothing. It appears above the Fabians in the historic Fabian Window, now held at the London School of Economics. LSE describes it as reflecting the Society’s gradualist approach.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-10)

Strictly speaking, historians often call it an emblem or coat of arms rather than a modern company logo. To ordinary people, the distinction changes nothing.

They chose a wolf hiding inside a harmless-looking sheep.

The image invites an obvious interpretation: present as moderate, move gradually and embed change through established institutions.

And the Society’s published method matches the image. A 2025 Fabian publication said the organisation was founded to permeate British institutions with evolutionary socialism.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-9)

In plain pub English, permeate means work your way inside.

Not through one dramatic revolution. Through Parliament, councils, universities, public bodies, policy networks and the institutions that shape national life.

The Society took its name from the Roman general Fabius, remembered for waiting, wearing down his opponent and striking at the right moment. Its own history reproduces the instruction to wait patiently, then strike hard.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-11)

The wolf is now a historic emblem rather than the Society's modern branding.

The gradual method remained.

THEY WRITE THE BLUEPRINTS — THEIR PEOPLE PUT THEM INTO PRACTICE

The Fabian Society does not merely comment on government from the sidelines.

It prepares plans for Labour to use once it holds power.

Its 2023 pamphlet was not coyly titled Suggestions for Discussion. It was called Plans for Power.

The pamphlet said that title was deliberate because many recommendations were things a new government should do after winning, rather than promises to place before voters during an election. It admitted that some proposals had already been adopted by Labour and called the rest a resource for a Labour government in power.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-8)

After the 2024 election, the Society’s annual report said its work had been woven into the Labour manifesto after years of dealing with shadow ministers. It also said Fabian staff gave detailed policy briefings to civil servants preparing for a change of government.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-6)

Once Labour entered office, the next annual report said Fabian staff were working with politicians and civil servants to help deliver the government’s missions. It described the Society as a leading organisation shaping government policy and celebrated its ideas and influence moving into the heart of government.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-7)

FABIAN RESEARCH→LABOUR MANIFESTO→FABIAN MINISTERS→CIVIL SERVICE DELIVERY

They prepare the plans. Labour adopts them. Their politicians take office. Civil servants are briefed to deliver them.

The public is then told it is simply “government policy”.

BELOW THE TOP, A PIPELINE FEEDS MORE OF THE SAME

The Fabian machine does not stop at the Prime Minister's office. Underneath it, a training pipeline keeps feeding the same network into public life.

The Fabian Women's Network said six women who had passed through its training programme were now MPs, two were working in No. 10 and two more were special advisers to Cabinet members — then added: the door to influencing policy is wide open and Fabian women are walking through it.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-7)

That is not an accusation from Reform UK. That is the Fabian network describing its own success. It is one visible piece of a wider pattern: the same organisation that sits with the Prime Minister also trains the people who go on to work beside him.

THIS IS NOT A FRINGE GROUP — IT IS THE BACKBONE OF LABOUR

The Fabian Society is often brushed off as one more harmless think tank.

That description is absurd.

After the 2024 general election, the Society celebrated 141 Labour MPs who were Fabian members. It said the prime minister and half the Cabinet were Fabians. It states that every Labour prime minister in history has belonged.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-3)[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-4)[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-5)

Its rules say the Society consists of socialists, exists to further socialism and is affiliated to the Labour Party. Full members must be eligible to join Labour, while members of parties that stand against Labour cannot remain full members.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-2)

So let us be precise: the modern Fabian Society is not a secret cross-party club containing Tories, Liberal Democrats and Reform members.

It is Labour’s machine.

Its people sit in Downing Street, the Cabinet, the Commons, the Lords, mayoral offices, councils and government advisory teams.

This is not a group whispering ideas into Labour’s ear.

It is the backbone running through the Labour Party from top to bottom.

BURNHAM, DOWNING STREET — AND THE FABIANS AROUND HIM

Andy Burnham became Labour leader on 16 July 2026 and Prime Minister on 20 July 2026. The earlier version of this article was written during the final days of Keir Starmer's premiership; that transition has now happened.

Burnham's current Fabian membership cannot be confirmed. The Society does not publish a complete public list of everyone who belongs, and we will not invent a membership card we cannot prove.

What is public is a long political relationship with Fabian platforms. Burnham has written for Fabian publications, contributed to a Fabian leadership pamphlet, addressed Fabian events and used Fabian platforms to set out political ideas.

One of the people Burnham chose to serve beside him in Greater Manchester was Kate Green — a former Fabian Society chair and a Fabian vice-president. Burnham appointed her as deputy mayor. The political picture has since moved on: Bev Craig became Mayor of Greater Manchester on 3 August 2026 and reappointed Green on 12 August 2026. Green remains Deputy Mayor for Safer and Stronger Communities.

The earlier article also recorded June 2026 press speculation that Green might take a No. 10 role if Burnham became Prime Minister. That was reporting about a possible appointment, not a confirmed one, and this updated version does not present it as fact.

THE FABIAN ESTABLISHMENT STRETCHES FAR BEYOND WESTMINSTER

Do not picture 141 unknown MPs meeting in a dusty back room.

The network reaches through powerful mayors, peers, union organisations, policy professionals, major companies, charities, universities, journalists and institutional figures.

Sadiq Khan, the Mayor of London, is a Fabian Society vice-president and a former chair. Other vice-presidents include Kate Green, Lord Kinnock, Lord Dubs, Baroness Hayter, Baroness Thornton, Dame Margaret Hodge and Seema Malhotra.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-11)[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-12)

The Society advertises private invitation-only expert roundtables and dinners. Its reports are distributed to members and key Westminster figures. It boasts one of the largest fringe programmes at Labour conference.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-25)

Its own conference programmes show ministers and Labour politicians sharing rooms with union leaders, senior journalists, think-tank directors, charity chiefs and representatives from finance and industry. Fabian events have been held in partnership with organisations including the TUC and Lloyds Banking Group, while programmes have featured KPMG, Experian, unions, broadcasters and major policy bodies.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-26)[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-27)

That does not make every partner a Fabian or prove that every person in the room agrees with Fabian socialism.

It proves something simpler: the Society has direct access across the circles where policy, money, organisation and public opinion meet.

A MEMBERSHIP LIST KEPT FROM PUBLIC VIEW — AND A NETWORK THAT REACHES EVERYWHERE

The Fabian Society publishes its staff, executive committee and vice-presidents. It does not publish a complete searchable list of all its thousands of members.

That is why Burnham’s exact membership status remains unconfirmed. The same uncertainty applies to many other people operating within its orbit.

What is visible is already extraordinary:

  • every Labour prime minister in history;
  • 141 Labour MPs after the 2024 election;
  • half the Cabinet at the start of the Starmer government;
  • powerful mayors and peers;
  • people working in No. 10 and beside Cabinet ministers;
  • links running from trade unions to high finance, public bodies, charities, academia and media.

Downing Street. City Hall. The Lords. The unions. The media. The banks. The institutions.

Almost everywhere public policy is made, financed, organised or sold to the country, the Fabian Society can point to members, partners, speakers or direct access.

This is not an ordinary think tank. It is a private political network running through the heart of the Labour establishment.

THE FABIAN ANTI-REFORM WAR ROOM

The Fabian Society does not merely feed Labour policy.

It is now openly helping Labour work out how to stop Reform UK.

A 2026 Fabian article urged Labour members and trade unionists to campaign against Reform online, in community groups and face to face. It said attacks over the NHS and workers’ rights must be made not once, but again and again, and called the fight against Reform the most important political struggle many readers would face.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-17)

Another Fabian article by Anneliese Dodds presented Reform as part of a global threat to women’s freedoms.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-18)

Then came the NHS scare campaign.

Wes Streeting used a Fabian conference speech to warn that the populist Right was coming for progressives and attacked Nigel Farage’s supposed “miserablist, declinist” vision. He turned the NHS into the battlefield on which Labour could defeat Reform.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-19)[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-29)

But Reform’s written 2024 policy said NHS services would remain free at the point of use. Its proposed voucher scheme would have sent patients for free private treatment when the NHS failed to meet waiting-time limits. Reform’s current policy again says the NHS will remain free at the point of use and funded through general taxation.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-20)[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-21)

The patient was never meant to receive a private bill.

Fabian stage.

Fabian minister.

Reform named as the enemy.

The NHS chosen as the weapon.

They help shape Labour’s policies — and Fabian writers and Fabian platforms help develop and amplify Labour’s attacks on the party threatening to remove it from power.

THE PLAN IS NOT ASSIMILATION — IT IS TO REMAKE BRITAIN

The Fabian platform does not merely argue that newcomers should be welcomed into British life.

It publishes arguments for changing British life, British institutions and British identity around them.

A 2024 Fabian article promoted multicultural nationalism, argued that equality required critically remaking Britishness, and called for a pluralistic thickening of national culture. It proposed drawing Islam and Judaism into a closer formal relationship with the established Church and described the programme as an addition to and remaking of national public culture.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-22)

A separate 2026 Fabian article called for a state-supported cultural shift through the BBC, national museums and public ceremonies, while arguing against Britain’s inherited Christian settlement.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-23)

The two writers do not agree on every detail. One route brings additional faiths further into state-backed national structures; another strips Christianity’s historic position away.

What both share is the belief that the state should deliberately reconstruct Britain’s identity from the top down.

Sadiq Khan’s Fabian pamphlet Fairness Not Favours proposed stronger engagement with British Muslim communities, greater Muslim representation in Labour and public life, faith-focused workplace policy and state action against Islamophobia.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-24)

Fabian publications formally carry a disclaimer that individual authors speak for themselves, not for a single collective Society policy. That matters and we state it plainly.

But it does not make the platform irrelevant. The Society chooses, approves, publishes, promotes and places these ideas before Labour politicians, activists and policy professionals.

DEI puts religious and group identity into institutional planning.

Multiculturalism replaces assimilation.

Public institutions are instructed to rewrite the national story.

They call it modern Britain.

Millions of ordinary people will see a country being rewritten without ever being asked.

LABOUR IS FABIAN TO ITS CORE — THE UNIPARTY PRESERVES ITS WORK

Again, let us keep the argument accurate.

Full Fabian membership is Labour-aligned. Conservatives, Liberal Democrats and Reform members cannot simply join as full members while standing against Labour.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-2)

The Uniparty argument begins after Fabian Labour puts the machinery in place.

Labour introduces the institutions, laws, funding streams, public bodies and accepted political language.

The Conservatives promise a change of direction but repeatedly leave much of the machinery standing.

The Liberal Democrats generally demand that it moves faster and further.

Then Labour returns and begins from the new starting point.

The Fabians do not need every rival politician to carry a Fabian membership card.

They only need the other establishment parties to preserve what Fabian Labour has already embedded.

Historically, the early Fabians openly tried to influence Liberals and Conservatives before helping found the Labour Party in 1900. Today, the membership rules are Labour-aligned — but the institutions and assumptions created over generations extend far beyond any one Labour government.[\[Evidence\]](https://britishlionsroar.github.io/fabian-puppet-show-british-lions-roar.html#src-11)

Labour builds it.

The Conservatives fail to remove it.

The Liberal Democrats demand more of it.

The permanent machine carries on.

THE CHOICE: THE PUPPET SHOW OR OPEN DEMOCRACY

The Fabian method depends upon most people never looking behind the curtain.

Plans are produced away from the election spotlight. People are trained and moved into positions of influence. Civil servants are briefed. Institutions are changed gradually. Ministers then present the finished product to the public.

Reform UK offers the opposite.

Its policies are published under its own name. Its arguments are made openly. Its candidates stand before the public and ask directly for their votes.

People may agree or disagree with those policies — but they can see who is proposing them and vote accordingly.

That is what democracy is meant to look like.

Not a private society with thousands of members but no complete public membership list.

Not “plans for power” written for use after the election.

Not an influence network running from Downing Street and City Hall to trade unions, advisers, public bodies, media figures and high finance.

Not a black wolf hiding inside white sheep’s clothing.

The Fabian Society has spent more than 140 years patiently building influence throughout British public life.

Labour carries the programme forward.

The Conservatives repeatedly fail to dismantle it.

The Liberal Democrats demand still more of it.

Reform UK now offers a democratic opportunity to break that cycle — through the ballot box, openly and peacefully.

The Fabian Society believes Britain should be reshaped by people working behind the curtain.

Reform UK believes the British people themselves should decide what sort of country they want.

The wolf has spent long enough among the sheep.
