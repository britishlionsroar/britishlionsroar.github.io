MODERATOR GUIDELINES

Everyday guide for Moderators & Admins
These guidelines are for everyday group moderation. Experienced admins may occasionally make exceptions for legitimate content.

CORE RULES

A — IF UNSURE, LEAVE IT

If you're not confident, don't approve, delete or block. Leave it for an experienced Moderator/Admin.

B — SOURCE REQUIRED

Facts, figures, statistics, news and breaking-news claims must have a URL to a reputable source.

Reliable sources include established mainstream media, official Reform UK/MP sources, government/public bodies and other identifiable reputable sources.

Unknown or made-up “news” sites and social-media pages/accounts are not acceptable sources.

C — PERSONAL ABUSE

No abusive, bullying or personally targeted posts, comments, images or memes.

Political satire is allowed.

There's a fine line between fun and personal abuse. If unsure, see A.

D — AGREEMENT DOESN'T OVERRIDE THE RULES

Don't approve something simply because you agree with its message.

It must still comply with the group rules and sourcing requirements.

1 — LANGUAGE & BEHAVIOUR

Not allowed

  • Swearing or obscene language, including words disguised with symbols or punctuation.
  • Hate speech, abusive name-calling or personal insults.
  • Calling people animals or making abusive comments about someone's nationality.

If it has already been posted, delete it. (See A & C)

2 — RESTRICTED SUBJECTS

Not allowed

  • Posts or comments about Tommy Robinson or Yellow Vests.
  • Posts or comments about vaccines or COVID.
  • Memes about Muslims, Islam or immigration.

Properly sourced news and legitimate posts concerning immigration may be allowed. (See A & B)

3 — FACTS, FIGURES & NEWS

Any post containing facts, figures, statistics, news or breaking-news claims MUST have a URL to a reputable source. (See B)

Not allowed

  • Unsupported facts or statistics.
  • Homemade memes/graphics presenting unsupported claims as fact.
  • Unknown or imitation “news” websites.
  • Social-media accounts/pages being used as the source of a factual/news claim.

Ordinary memes, opinions and general political posts do not require a source URL unless they contain factual, statistical or news claims.

4 — VIDEOS

ALLOWED: Only from YouTube or reliable sources such as mainstream media. (Approx. 10 min max length)

NOT ALLOWED: Videos from X, TikTok, Instagram or other social-media accounts.

YouTube videos from unknown individuals making questionable factual claims should not be approved. (See A & B)

5 — IMAGES & MEMES

Not allowed

  • Nudity or excessive violence.
  • Images of death or instruments of death, including ropes, guillotines, knives or guns.
  • Nazi symbols or imagery suggesting Nazi associations.
  • Abusive or bullying-style images/memes aimed at an individual. Political satire is allowed. (See A & C)
  • Photos/collages of convicted grooming gangs etc. without a reputable source and link to the actual news story. (See B)

6 — RIOTS, VIOLENCE & PROTESTS

Not allowed

  • Posts, videos or images covering riots, violence or serious disorder.
  • Content promoting, organising, encouraging or glorifying violent or potentially confrontational protests/demonstrations.

Peaceful community/local protests may be allowed.

Legitimate reporting from established news sources may also be allowed. (See A & B)

7 — FUNDRAISING & PETITIONS

NOT ALLOWED: Fundraising, requests for money or petitions.

This applies regardless of the cause.

8 — KEYWORD ALERTS

Check the flagged comment.

OK → RELEASE

BREAKS THE RULES → DELETE

UNSURE → SEE A

9 — SPAM

DELETE obvious spam.

If Facebook has incorrectly placed a legitimate post in Spam, check the content and any source before releasing it. (See A & B)

10 — REPORTED POSTS & COMMENTS

A member reporting something doesn't automatically mean it should be deleted.

Check it against the rules.

OK → LEAVE/RELEASE

BREAKS THE RULES → DELETE

Someone clearly trolling or deliberately disrupting the group may need to be blocked.

Always make sure you're blocking the correct person. (See A)

11 — DECLINING POSTS

When declining a pending post, use the appropriate Group Rule as the reason wherever possible.

NEED HELP?

If you have a question or aren't sure about something, please ask in any of the Moderator/Admin chats you've been added to.

There is no such thing as a silly question. We'd much rather you ask than guess.
