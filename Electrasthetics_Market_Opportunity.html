★ The Market Gap ★

Premium Conversions.
Mismatched Fittings.

The market already contains capable electrical components. What it lacks is a coordinated, design-led system for presenting those components cleanly across modern campervan interiors.

Domestic switches, a black rocker, a stuck-on USB charger — the mismatched reality of most current installations

The components already exist. The opportunity is to make them look like they belong together.

The Market Gap
Premium conversions still finish with mismatched plastic fittings.

Builders may spend heavily on cabinetry, upholstery, appliances, lighting and electrical systems, yet the visible controls are often assembled from unrelated products with no shared aesthetic. This is not a niche styling complaint — it is a recurring finish-quality problem across self-builds, professional conversions and specialist vehicle interiors.

Why Now
The electrical system has become more visible, more complex.

Campervans now carry more electrical equipment than ever — solar, lithium batteries, inverters, heating, connectivity, monitoring, smart lighting and multiple charging points. That increases the number of controls in the living space, and makes poor visual integration more noticeable.

01

More Devices

Every additional electrical function creates another visible control, socket or indicator.

02

Higher Expectations

Customers compare vehicle interiors with premium domestic, marine and automotive design.

03

More Custom Builds

Self-builders and converters increasingly seek products that reflect the style of the vehicle.

Current Failure Points
Existing products solve functions. Not the finished installation.

This is what a typical current installation looks like once several systems have been added over time — the exact pattern Electrasthetics is designed to replace.

A real interior showing the pattern: mismatched switch styles, exposed screws, mixed finishes, trailing wires and trunking

What Installers Face Now

❌ Different sizes and bezel depths
❌ Visible front screws
❌ Black-only or restricted colours
❌ No shared mounting architecture
❌ Direct cutting into finished furniture
❌ Limited access for later servicing
❌ No coordinated mixed-module system

What Electrasthetics Provides

✓ One recognisable housing language
✓ Concealed fixing and clean removal
✓ Interior-friendly colour options
✓ Common backplates and adapters
✓ Surface mounting where needed
✓ Serviceable, replaceable modules
✓ Expandable compatibility over time
Commercial Positioning
A premium finishing system — not a commodity backbox.

Electrasthetics should not compete purely on price with generic plastic enclosures. Its value is the improvement it brings to the complete installation — sitting between low-cost universal fittings and expensive bespoke fabrication.

A

Design-Led

The customer buys a cleaner finished interior, not simply a box around a switch.

B

System-Based

Each product strengthens the value of the complete family and encourages repeat selection.

C

Installer-Friendly

Concealed fixing, modular inserts and service access support practical installation as well as appearance.
