Chapter 02

Market Opportunity

The market already contains capable electrical components. What it lacks is a coordinated, design-led system for presenting those components cleanly across modern campervan interiors.

Visible value. The opportunity sits in the fittings customers see, touch and judge every day.
On this page The market gap Why now Customer groups Current failure points Commercial positioning
01
The market gap

Premium conversions still finish with mismatched plastic fittings.

Builders may spend heavily on cabinetry, upholstery, appliances, lighting and electrical systems, yet the visible controls are often assembled from unrelated products with no shared aesthetic.

The result is familiar: a black rocker switch beside a white USB socket, a separate heater controller, exposed screw heads, a deep plastic box and a cable entry that looks added after everything else was finished.

This is not a niche styling complaint. It is a recurring finish-quality problem across self-builds, professional conversions and specialist vehicle interiors.

02
Why now

The electrical system has become more visible, more complex and more important.

Campervans now carry more electrical equipment than ever: solar, lithium batteries, inverters, heating, connectivity, monitoring, smart lighting and multiple charging points.

That increases the number of controls and fittings in the living space. It also makes poor visual integration more noticeable. Customers increasingly expect a finished interior to feel like one designed environment rather than a collection of aftermarket parts.

01

More devices

Every additional electrical function creates another visible control, socket or indicator.

02

Higher expectations

Customers compare vehicle interiors with premium domestic, marine and automotive design.

03

More custom builds

Self-builders and converters increasingly seek products that reflect the style of the vehicle.

03
Current failure points

Existing products solve individual functions, not the finished installation.

What installers face now

  • Different sizes and bezel depths
  • Visible front screws
  • Black-only or restricted colours
  • No shared mounting architecture
  • Direct cutting into finished furniture
  • Limited access for later servicing
  • No coordinated mixed-module system

What Electrasthetics can provide

  • One recognisable housing language
  • Concealed fixing and clean removal
  • Interior-friendly colour options
  • Common backplates and adapters
  • Surface mounting where needed
  • Serviceable, replaceable modules
  • Expandable compatibility over time

04
Commercial positioning

A premium finishing system—not a commodity backbox.

Electrasthetics should not compete purely on price with generic plastic enclosures. Its value is the improvement it brings to the complete installation.

The products can sit between low-cost universal fittings and expensive bespoke fabrication: affordable enough for repeat use, but distinct enough to justify a premium over generic components.

A

Design-led

The customer buys a cleaner finished interior, not simply a box around a switch.

B

System-based

Each product strengthens the value of the complete family and encourages repeat selection.

C

Installer-friendly

Concealed fixing, modular inserts and service access support practical installation as well as appearance.

The strongest opportunity is to become the recognised finishing system for visible vehicle electrics.
