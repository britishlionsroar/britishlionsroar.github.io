Development Path
A measured route from concept to first products.

Rather than committing to the full range at once, the recommended path is to select a small number of high-value products, prototype and test them properly, then expand once that first phase is proven.

Step 01

Select

Choose the strongest first products by market gap and cross-range value.

Step 04

Launch & Extend

Release a coherent starter range, then add compatible housings.

Working Together
What each side brings to this.

Electrasthetics Brings

  • A complete, coordinated design system already applied across 13 products
  • Detailed concept documentation for every proposed item in this range
  • A shared closure, insert and finish architecture that keeps new development cost low
  • A clear, staged development path rather than an all-or-nothing launch

Needed From Anvil

  • Selection of which products to prioritise for the first development phase
  • Manufacturer-verified dimensions for any third-party components not yet confirmed
  • Feedback from real installation experience to refine fit and service access
  • Agreement on production route — in-house 3D printing, outsourced, or a mix
