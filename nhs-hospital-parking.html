Abolishing NHS Hospital Parking Charges

Official policy announcement · 27 November 2026 · England

Independent library summary of two Reform UK publications, not a separately issued party policy paper.

Commitment

Reform UK proposes removing parking fees at NHS hospitals in England for patients, visitors and staff. A dedicated Free Parking Grant would replace lost trust income, with existing barriers and cameras retained to prevent misuse and private parking contracts phased out.

Funding

The party reports £300 million in parking receipts for 2025/26: £213.4 million from patients and visitors and £86.6 million from staff. It estimates annual implementation costs of £285 million and additional overseas visitor healthcare revenue of £303 million. These are party estimates, not independently verified outcomes.

The proposal includes expanded charging and recovery of overseas visitor bills, and mandatory travel insurance covering NHS treatment for visitors from countries without reciprocal healthcare arrangements, enforced through visa and ETA systems.

Wider NHS commitment

Robert Jenrick also committed to real-terms increases in NHS funding, with broader spending plans to be announced nearer the general election.
