The original requests covered just six foreign nationalities: Afghan, Albanian, Eritrean, Iranian, Iraqi and Syrian. Some police forces supplied additional ethnicity or country-of-birth information.

Here we publish the police figures and their original FOI sources. Our continuing investigation will examine the public costs and the available evidence concerning victims of sexual and violent offences. Those findings will be published separately when verified.

About the figures

These are police-reported FOI figures, not a count of all arrests of non-British people. The original requests covered six specified nationalities; some disclosures instead use country of birth, ethnicity, detainees or offence records. Reporting periods vary. Arrests do not establish guilt and are not convictions.

* Essex's 80,432 figure covers violence and sexual offence records across broader ethnicity categories, including White categories, and is not comparable with the six-nationality arrest figures. Some forces count dual nationalities more than once; some published totals are minimums. Source pages give the original definitions. Map excludes forces with no supplied numeric figure.
