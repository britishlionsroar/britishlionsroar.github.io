Chapter 01

Executive Summary

Electrasthetics is a proposed family of design-led electrical housings and installation accessories for campervans, motorhomes and specialist vehicles—created to solve the visible mismatch between high-quality electrical systems and poorly coordinated interior fittings.

One system. Multiple product categories, one recognisable visual language and a commercially expandable platform.

01
The opportunity

Vehicle electrics are engineered as systems—but presented as unrelated parts.

Modern campervan interiors increasingly use premium materials, carefully coordinated colours and high-value appliances. Yet the switches, sockets, controllers, lights and cable fittings remain visually fragmented.

Most visible electrical components are designed around manufacturing convenience or domestic and automotive conventions. Converters therefore assemble finished interiors from products with different dimensions, colours, corner radii, fixing methods and bezel depths.

The gap is not a lack of electrical hardware. It is the lack of a coordinated system for presenting that hardware properly.

02
The proposition

A modular exterior architecture around proven electrical components.

Electrasthetics does not need to reinvent every switch, socket or control. Its value lies in creating original housings, adapter systems and installation products that allow those components to share one premium visual identity.

The core strategy is to separate the visible enclosure from the internal electrical module. Common backplates, interchangeable inserts and concealed-fixing outer housings create a system that can expand across multiple applications without starting again each time.

This turns a series of individual accessories into a repeatable product ecosystem.

The proposed range

Built around products installers already need.

The first range combines universal architecture with specialist housings for recurring campervan installation problems.

01

Universal modular housings

Shared enclosures and inserts for round sockets, USB modules, rocker switches, push buttons and related controls.

02

Surface-mounted backboxes

Clean, concealed-fixing housings for installations where cutting finished cabinetry is undesirable or impossible.

03

Modular control panels

Coordinated mixed-control arrangements capable of presenting complete electrical functions as one system.

04

Smart downlight housings

Surface enclosures that allow modern low-profile recessed fittings to become intentional interior features.

05

Specialist equipment housings

Purpose-designed solutions for heater controllers, routers, Wema instruments and compact security viewers.

06

Cable-management details

Visible routing products designed to finish installations rather than merely conceal the worst of them.

03
Commercial rationale

Commercial strength comes from the family—not only the first product.

A converter who adopts one Electrasthetics fitting has a reason to select matching products elsewhere in the same vehicle. Every additional insert or specialist housing increases the usefulness of the full system.

A

Repeat purchasing

A coordinated range encourages multiple fittings per installation and future additions.

B

Installer standardisation

Converters can build a familiar installation method into repeat vehicle designs.

C

Expandable compatibility

New adapter inserts can widen the range without replacing its core architecture.

04
Development path

Start narrow, validate properly, then expand the system.

The recommended approach is to select a small number of high-value products for measured development and real-world prototype testing.

Initial manufacture can use additive processes to prove geometry, retention, wiring access and customer demand. Once a product achieves reliable volume, its production method can be reviewed independently without changing the broader product language.

STEP 01

Select

Choose the strongest first products based on market gap, development complexity and cross-range value.

STEP 02

Measure and prototype

Build from physical samples, white papers and verified component dimensions.

STEP 03

Install and refine

Test fit, service access, retention, cable routing, finish quality and installer experience.

STEP 04

Launch and extend

Release a coherent starter range, then add compatible inserts and specialist housings.
