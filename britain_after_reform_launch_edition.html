London — Sunday 10 June 2029

The final constituency has declared, the counting is over and Britain has delivered the greatest political upheaval in its modern democratic history.

Reform UK has won 432 of the 650 seats in the House of Commons, giving Nigel Farage a commanding overall majority of 214 and the power to form a government without coalition partners, confidence agreements or support from any of the old parties.

Labour, the Conservatives and the Liberal Democrats — the three parties of the Uniparty — have been decisively rejected.

No coalition. No compromise deal. No excuses.

The New Government
10 June 2029
The morning after the landslide

A Majority Large Enough to Deliver the Contract

Westminster — Sunday morning

Nigel Farage is expected to travel to Buckingham Palace this morning and accept the King’s invitation to form Britain’s first Reform UK government.

The scale of the result means there will be no negotiations with Labour, no deal with the Conservatives and no Liberal Democrat demands attached to a coalition agreement.

Reform has the votes to govern alone, pass its programme and survive even substantial rebellions from within its own ranks.

The incoming Prime Minister is expected to begin appointing his Cabinet immediately, with the first senior posts announced before the end of the day and the full ministerial team taking shape on Monday.

Whitehall departments have been told to prepare rapid implementation papers covering the first week, first 30 days and first 100 days of the new administration.

The first King’s Speech is expected to place border control, deportation, constitutional reform, tax cuts, policing, energy and the reduction of government waste at the centre of the legislative programme.

Reform officials have stressed throughout the campaign that Our Contract with You is not one enormous Bill. Its promises will require separate legislation, Budget measures, executive instructions and departmental action.

The government’s first test will therefore be speed without chaos: introducing urgent measures quickly while ensuring that every change can survive Parliament, the courts and the permanent machinery of the state.

The Result Is Already Changing Behaviour

Channel

Zero Small-Boat Arrivals Recorded Since Reform Victory

No successful Channel arrivals have been recorded in the first 48 hours since the result. Officials cautioned that weather and short-term disruption may be factors, but smugglers are reported to be reassessing promises that passengers will be allowed to remain in Britain.

France

Calais Camps Begin to Thin as Britain Route Is Reconsidered

French charities and local officials report early movement away from makeshift camps near the coast. Some migrants are said to be considering other destinations or returning along the routes by which they entered France.

Travel

One-Way Flight Bookings Surge After Deportation Pledge

Airlines have reported an unusual rise in outbound one-way searches and bookings. Travel agents say the increase appears concentrated among people uncertain whether they will retain a lawful right to remain once enforcement operations begin.

Westminster

Peers Prepare to Fight Plan to Replace House of Lords

Senior peers are preparing a parliamentary campaign against Reform’s proposal for a much smaller and more democratic second chamber. Ministers are expected to begin the reform process within the government’s first 100 days.

Whitehall

Departments Ordered to Produce 100-Day Delivery Plans

Permanent secretaries have been instructed to identify the laws, spending decisions, contracts and staffing changes required to begin delivering the published programme at once.

Why this series exists

A Future Report Built on Real Published Policies

Many Reform supporters know what they want changed but have never sat down and read a full policy document. Britain After Reform turns those commitments into short future news reports showing what they might look like when carried out by a majority government.

The election result, dates, Cabinet appointments and events are fictional. The policy quotations are real. Each later report will focus on one main promise and end with the exact published wording and its official source.

Where Reform UK has issued a newer formal policy that expands or replaces wording in the 2024 Contract, the newer official policy will be used and identified.

Our Contract with You

“Our Contract with You is not just another party manifesto. It sets out the reforms that Britain needs in the first 100 days following a general election and thereafter.”

Immigration and the boats

“Illegal migrants who come to the UK will be detained and deported.”

House of Lords reform

“Replace the crony-filled House of Lords with a much smaller, more democratic second chamber.”

Current official immigration policy

“A Reform government will stop the boats by immediately leaving the ECHR, restoring full control of our borders, intercepting and detaining all illegal arrivals, and deporting them.”
