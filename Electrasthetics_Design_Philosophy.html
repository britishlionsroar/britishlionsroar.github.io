★ How Every Product Is Built ★

One Design Language.
Applied Consistently.

Electrasthetics is not a single product — it is a set of design rules applied to every housing, panel and fitting in the range, so that anything wearing the Electrasthetics name looks and behaves the same way.

Design Intent
Every product follows the same six rules.

Rather than designing each housing from scratch, every Electrasthetics product is built against a shared set of design rules. This is what makes the range feel like one coordinated system rather than a collection of unrelated parts, and what allows new products to be added later without breaking that consistency.

01

Concealed Fixing

No visible screws on any finished, closed product. Fixing points are hidden inside the assembly.

02

Flush Closure

Every closed housing sits flush — nothing protruding, no visible gaps or indentations at the seams.

03

Shared Geometry

Corner radii, edge profiles and proportions are common across the range, not reinvented per product.

04

Serviceable

Products can be opened and closed again for maintenance without tools or damage to the housing.

05

Interior-Friendly Finish

Colours and textures are chosen to sit naturally inside a finished vehicle interior, not industrial defaults.

06

Printable as Standard

Every product is designed to be produced by 3D printing as the default manufacturing method.

The rules don't change. Only the product wrapped around them does.

Closure Mechanism
3D printed cantilever snap hook closures — and, where required, lower hooking lugs.

Every Electrasthetics housing closes using 3D printed cantilever snap hook closures, and, where required, lower hooking lugs. Both features are printed as part of the backplate and casing themselves — they are not separate hardware to be purchased, sourced or fitted. The exact number and position of closure points varies by product, based on the shape and function of each individual case.

Materials & Finishes
A shared palette, not a per-product decision.

Colour and finish options are shared across the whole range, so a customer fitting several Electrasthetics products together always has a matching set to choose from.

Range Identity
Recognisable as a family, not a coincidence.

Because every product shares the same design rules, closure logic and finish options, products from different parts of the range read as belonging together when installed side by side — without needing matching labels or branding to prove it.

A

Consistent Silhouette

Shared corner radii and proportions make every product instantly recognisable as Electrasthetics.

B

Expandable Without Redesign

New products adopt the same rules from day one, rather than needing to be retrofitted to match later.

C

Installer Confidence

One closure logic and one fixing approach across the range means less to learn per product.

The rules are the range. Every product that follows them belongs.
