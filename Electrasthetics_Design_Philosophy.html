Design Philosophy

The principles behind every Electrasthetics product.

Objective: Every visible electrical component inside a campervan should appear to belong to one coordinated interior system rather than a collection of unrelated products.

Concealed Installation

Where practical, products are designed around hidden fixing methods to minimise visible screws and create cleaner, furniture-grade installations.

Modular By Design

Instead of creating completely different housings for every application, Electrasthetics uses common design language and shared construction methods, allowing future products to expand naturally into the ecosystem.

Consistency
Shared styling across every product family.
Compatibility
Designed to complement established industry equipment rather than replace it.
Scalability
New products can be added without redesigning the complete range.
Manufacturability
Optimised for additive manufacturing, rapid prototyping and continuous refinement.

Colour Coordination

The range is intended to be available in finishes more suited to modern camper interiors than the traditional "any colour as long as it's black" approach. Neutral colours allow electrical fittings to blend naturally with cabinetry rather than dominate it.

Designed For Growth

The products presented within this proposal represent the beginning of a much broader ecosystem. Every housing, mounting solution and finishing accessory has been conceived with future expansion in mind, enabling Electrasthetics to evolve into a recognisable product family.
